Remodel Design Lead

Josias Edouard

Josias leads Genius Creative's redesign practice — full creative remodels for clubs and restaurants across Scottsdale and Phoenix, built around bold, original art direction rather than off-the-shelf design.

Approach

Redesign, led by an artist's eye

Josias treats every venue like a canvas — walking a space before proposing a single material, and building the concept, murals, and layout together instead of layering art on top of a finished room. His projects are led entirely by the needs of the client's crowd and brand, from late-night lounges to full-service dining rooms.

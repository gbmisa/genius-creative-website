Charcoal & oil paint on canvas

Femme de la Nuit

By Josias Yisra'el

24 × 36 in

A single figure caught mid-transformation — human features dissolving into something wilder at the edges. Painted in charcoal and oil across a stretch of late-night client walkthroughs, it became the mood board for an after-hours lounge concept before the paint had finished drying.

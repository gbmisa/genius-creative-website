About the Studio

Genius Creative

A Scottsdale/Phoenix studio built on one idea: the same visual language that makes a painting worth collecting can make a room worth remembering.

The Story

Fine art, at every scale

Genius Creative started as a fine art practice — bold, saturated, psychedelic-leaning paintings meant to hold a room's attention. That same instinct is now applied to the clubs and restaurants of Scottsdale and Phoenix: full creative remodels where the art isn't decoration added at the end, it's the starting point.

The studio is built around three people, each bringing a different piece of that vision to life.
